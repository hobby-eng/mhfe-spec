### Informational observations and recommendations

- Obtain independent cryptographic analysis of state-derived salts, reduced-call filters,
  multi-target attacks and the narrower Suite 4 state; no exploit was established here.
- Treat deniability as conditional on the stated synthetic experiment; real ledger correlations,
  source conditioning, related containers and side channels need separate analysis.
- Prefer an independently held receiving address when actual wallet identity matters; the 16-bit
  source predicate and 32-bit fingerprint have stated probabilistic limits.
- Keep repair cards and password-check material private as specified; their disclosed entropy losses
  are deliberate tradeoffs, not new findings.
- Verify the current mhfe implementation against current recovery/re-encryption rules; the
  specification already documents the v0.6.0 ambiguous-rehearsal difference.

### Assessment and limitations

- Standard security/design scan with targeted verification, not a full release audit or independent
  cryptographic certification.
- No new full-cost Argon2 replay, GPU/ASIC benchmark, side-channel experiment, PRP/SPRP proof or
  optimal attack-cost proof was performed.
- Historical audit reports were checked for schema and paired IDs, not for the truth of every
  historical finding, source hash or claimed test result.
- External citations, release publication and remote vulnerability-reporting settings were not
  checked; source reviewers stayed offline.
- Recorded-key replay verifies packing, salt/mask messages, Feistel equations and encodings, but
  cannot establish the recorded Argon2 outputs.
- The optional-profile probes use Python Unicode 16 on stable public inputs; the vector investigator
  separately checks the frozen Unicode-17 cases with the pinned Node runtime.
